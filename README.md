# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Data Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Vrinda Store Data Analysis** project is an end-to-end **Excel data analytics and dashboard project** focused on analyzing sales performance, customer behavior, order trends, and channel performance for Vrinda Store.

The project uses an annual sales dataset containing **31,000+ orders** and transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights using **Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, and data visualization techniques**.

The objective is to understand **who the customers are, what they purchase, where sales come from, and how the business can improve its sales performance**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Vrinda Store wants to understand its sales performance and customer purchasing behavior in order to make better business decisions.

The analysis focuses on:

* 📈 Monthly sales and order trends
* 👩‍🦰👨 Customer gender distribution
* 🎂 Age-group purchasing behavior
* 📍 State-wise sales performance
* 🛒 Sales across different online channels
* 📦 Order status distribution
* 💰 Revenue and order performance
* 🧾 Product category and quantity analysis
* 🔎 Identification of high-performing customer segments

## 📈 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales vs Orders

Analyzed monthly revenue and order volume to understand sales trends throughout the year.

Key metrics include:

* Total Sales Amount
* Number of Orders
* Monthly Sales
* Monthly Order Volume

---

### 2. Men vs Women Analysis

Compared purchasing behavior between male and female customers.

The analysis helps identify:

* Which gender contributes more revenue
* Customer distribution by gender
* Gender-based purchasing trends

---

### 3. Age & Gender Analysis

Analyzed customers based on:

* Age
* Age Group
* Gender
* Number of Orders

This helps identify the most valuable customer segments.

---

### 4. Order Status Analysis

Orders were analyzed according to their status, including:

* Delivered
* Cancelled
* Returned
* Refunded

This provides insight into successful deliveries and order losses.

---

### 5. State-wise Sales Analysis

Analyzed revenue across different Indian states to identify the strongest geographic markets.

This helps answer:

> Which states generate the highest sales for Vrinda Store?

---

### 6. Sales Channel Analysis

Compared order performance across different online marketplaces/channels such as:

* Amazon
* Myntra
* Ajio
* Flipkart
* Meesho
* Others

The analysis identifies which channels contribute the highest number of orders.
